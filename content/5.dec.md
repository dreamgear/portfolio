*Operations Analyst 1982–1988 | Computer Operator 1980–1982*

- Rose through the ranks from an entry-level Computer Operator to Software Engineer, consistently expanding technical scope and mastering emerging technologies over an 18-year tenure.
- TOPS-10, TOPS-20, RSTS/E, VMS, and Ultrix from Batch with 9 Track tapes to Engineering Workstations.
- Implemented Remote System Management tool for VMS engineering workstations, and became local expert.
- Implemented DECAthena, MIT's distributed computing suite, including the components that came before Active Directory: Kerberos, Hesiod/DNS, Zephyr Messaging, and Distributed file systems, and became a local expert.
