One of things I want to be able to do is tweak my portfolio site, and add blog entries like this one from a browser, without having a real development environment.  I knew about vscode.dev, which give you vscode in a browser, but I've recently learned about github.dev, which gives you vscode in a browser with baked-in github integration.

It's just the ticket for this kind of a task.
