After more than twenty years working in enterprise software, I've seen technologies come and go. Mainframes gave way to client-server, which gave way to the web, which is now giving way to cloud-native and AI-assisted development.

## What Changed

The tooling is unrecognizably better. Version control, CI/CD, containerization, and Infrastructure as Code have transformed how we ship software. A single developer today can accomplish what took entire teams in the early 2000s.

## What Stayed the Same

The fundamentals haven't changed. Understanding data flow, writing clear code, communicating with stakeholders, and thinking about failure modes — these skills are timeless. No framework or AI tool replaces the need for solid engineering judgment.

## Looking Forward

I'm most excited about AI-assisted development. Tools like Antigravity are changing how I write code daily. The key is treating AI as a collaborator, not a replacement — it amplifies your expertise rather than substituting for it.
